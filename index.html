CASE STUDY 01 / SEARCH PERFORMANCE

Diagnosing visibility without proportional clicks

Here's the lesson greatEvent taught me: visibility and traffic aren't the same thing. I used query-level Search Console data to find pages that were already earning impressions and rankings but weren't converting that visibility into clicks.

MetricEvent Space Costs pageWhy it mattered
Impressions70,745Strong search visibility
Clicks401Traffic opportunity remained
CTR0.6%Primary optimization signal
Average position5.2Page-one visibility

Queries I investigated

party hall rentals near mehall rentals near me rental halls near meevent rental prices

What I did

  • Reviewed query-level impressions, clicks, CTR, and average position.
  • Mapped visible queries against the page's actual topic and search intent.
  • Separated informational, commercial, and transactional intent.
  • Rewrote title and meta to match the language people were actually searching.
  • Used internal links to strengthen topical relationships.
  • Monitored performance post-optimization and let new query data guide the next round.
Key lesson: a top ranking isn't the finish line. I look at whether the result earns the click and whether the page actually satisfies the intent behind it.
CASE STUDY 02 / CONTENT SEO

From keyword research to a search-led content brief

I use a repeatable workflow to turn keyword opportunities into content with a clear job to do — not just another article stuffed around a keyword.

Workflow

  • Identify primary and related search terms.
  • Classify search intent and SERP type.
  • Review competing page structures for missing subtopics.
  • Map the primary keyword to the right page type.
  • Build a heading structure around real user questions.
  • Define internal links to and from supporting pages.
  • Add FAQ, comparison, pricing, or location sections where intent calls for it.
  • Publish, monitor GSC, refresh based on performance.

CASE STUDY 03 / ON-PAGE + TECHNICAL SEO

Maintaining SEO hygiene across multiple page types

For a marketplace with blogs, location pages, venue pages, and service categories, hygiene needs repeatable checks, not one-off audits.

Checks I work with

  • Indexation and Search Console coverage signals.
  • Title tags and meta descriptions.
  • Internal-link opportunities and orphaned pages.
  • Broken-link identification and prioritization.
  • Keyword overlap and content cannibalization.
  • Heading structure and content hierarchy.
  • Schema and structured-data opportunities.
  • Local SEO consistency across location pages.

Execution mindset

Prioritize by likely organic impact, document what needs to change, ship or coordinate the fix, then go back to Search Console and check whether it actually worked.
