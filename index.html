About

I'm naturally curious about how people search — what they type when they're confused, what makes them click one result over another, and why some pages earn trust and others don't. I like turning messy Search Console data into practical growth decisions.

Most of my experience comes from owning organic acquisition for a US events marketplace: building topical authority through content clusters, strengthening internal linking so crawl budget goes to pages that convert, and writing for how people search now — including inside Google's AI Overviews, not just the ten blue links.

I've also built two channels from zero — Reddit and Pinterest — using real user conversations and audience data to shape what gets written, not just what ranks. I enjoy the experimentation side of this work as much as the execution: testing a new content structure, a new internal-linking pattern, or a new way to earn an AI citation, then watching what the data says.

How I work

My SEO process

The same seven-step loop, run on every content cluster — from first keyword to next refresh.

01Research

Search Console, Pinterest, Reddit and Trends data to find real demand.

→
02Search intent

Map what the searcher actually wants — informational, commercial, or navigational.

→
03Content strategy

Cluster topics into blogs, FAQs, and landing pages that support each other.

→
04On-page SEO

Titles, meta, schema, and structure written for both readers and AI Overviews.

→
05Internal linking

Route authority and crawl budget toward the pages that convert.

→
06Performance tracking

Weekly Search Console and GA4 review — clicks, CTR, and position by page.

→
07Content refresh

Rewrite underperforming pages using what the data revealed. Loop back to 01.
